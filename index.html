Dear Kritika, ♡

Kritika,

Okay, before you say anything — yes, I actually put effort into making this.

And honestly, there are a lot of things I could say about our friendship, but maybe the easiest place to start is December 2024.

That's when our friendship started.

I don't think either of us knew where it would go.

And somehow, here we are.

We've had good days, stupid conversations, ridiculous jokes and some genuinely great memories.

We've also had fights.

A lot of them.

Sometimes over the dumbest things imaginable.

There have been moments when we've both been annoyed, irritated, stubborn and probably convinced that the other person was impossible.

But somehow...

we always fix it.

And I think that's one of my favourite things about us.

Our friendship isn't special because we never fight.

It's special because we don't let the fights become the end.

And I want to admit something too.

You have probably put more effort into this friendship than I have.

You've tried to fix things when I was being stubborn.

You've cared about things even when I didn't realise how much they mattered.

You've made the effort to keep the friendship going.

And I don't say thank you for that nearly enough.

So...

thank you.

For staying.

For caring.

For putting up with me.

For all the random conversations.

For every laugh.

For every argument that somehow became another story we laugh about later.

And for being the kind of person who doesn't just walk away when things get difficult.

On your birthday, I genuinely hope you get everything you deserve.

I hope this year gives you reasons to smile more, worry less, dream bigger and enjoy every little thing.

I hope you meet amazing people, experience amazing things and create memories that you'll remember for years.

And I hope our friendship gets a lot more memories too.

More random conversations.

More stupid jokes.

More arguments that we'll eventually laugh about.

More photos.

More adventures.

More everything.

Because December 2024 was only the beginning.

So happy birthday, Kritika.

Thank you for being my girl bestie.

And yes...

unfortunately for you,

you're stuck with me.
